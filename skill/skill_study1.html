Guglielmo & Malle "Enough Skill to Kill"
Study 1

Slip After condition:

Jake desperately wants to have more money. He knows that he will inherit a lot of money when his aunt dies. One day Jake sees his aunt walking by the window. He raises his rifle, gets her in the sights, and pulls the trigger. But Jake isn't very good at using his rifle. His hand slips on the barrel of the gun, the shot goes wild, and bounces off a heavy post. Nonetheless, the bullet hits his aunt directly in the heart. She dies instantly.

1. Did Jake kill his aunt intentionally?

no yes

Slip Before condition:

Jake desperately wants to have more money. He knows that he will inherit a lot of money when his aunt dies. One day Jake sees his aunt walking by the window. He raises his rifle, gets her in the sights, but his hand slips on the barrel of the gun even before he pulls the trigger. Nonetheless, somehow a shot gets off and the bullet hits his aunt directly in the heart. She dies instantly.

1. Did Jake kill his aunt intentionally?

no yes

Bouncing off a nearby post, the bullet nonetheless hit his aunt directly in the heart. She died instantly.
