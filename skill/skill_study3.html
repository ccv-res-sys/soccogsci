Guglielmo & Malle "Enough Skill to Kill"
Study 3

Common information:

George and his sister, Lena, reunite at Thanksgiving at their parents’ house. Whereas George just got into medical school, Lena has problems in her marriage and just lost her job.

Over the course of the day, George and Lena get into a number of arguments, some political, some about relationships. Later in the afternoon they play a game of darts. They split the first two games, and the third game is close till the end, because neither of them is any good at the game. Who will win comes down to George’s last shot. If he hits 11 or more, he wins; if he hits less than 11, Lena wins.

Negative Skill condition:

George doesn’t care that Lena is having a difficult time; he really wants to beat her. He aims the dart at a region of the board in which he would probably make more than 11 points. He sets up his shot and...the dart lands in the 14-point segment. George triumphs in his victory while Lena is sad.

Negative Low-skill condition:

George doesn’t care that Lena is having a difficult time; he really wants to beat her. However, George has little control over his shots. He tries to aim at a region of the darts board in which he would probably make more than 11 points. As he sets up his shot, however, he loses his balance, the dart slips out of his hand and wobbles toward the board, and...the dart lands in the 14-point segment. George triumphs in his victory while Lena is sad.

Positive Skill condition:

George thinks of the difficult time Lena is having, and he really wants to let her win. He aims the dart at a region of the board in which he would probably make fewer than 11 points. He sets up his shot and...the dart lands in the 6-point segment. Lena wins the game and is very happy.

Positive Low-skill condition:

George thinks of the difficult time Lena is having, and he really wants to let her win. However, George has little control over his shots. He tries to aim at a region of the darts board in which he would probably make fewer than 11 points. As he sets up his shot, however, he loses his balance, the dart slips out of his hand and wobbles toward the board, and...the dart lands in the 6-point segment. Lena wins the game and is very happy.
