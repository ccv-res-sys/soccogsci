Study 2

Skill condition:

Frank T. had an ongoing dispute with one of his neighbors over a stretch of land that both men had claimed for years. The disputes became increasingly hostile when the neighbor repeatedly drove a tractor on the disputed land.

Frank T. decided to teach his neighbor a lesson. He waited until he spotted the neighbor driving onto the disputed land, raised a rifle, and aimed at the neighbor's body. Frank T. was an expert marksman, so he pulled the trigger and directly hit the neighbor's body, causing significant injury.

Low skill condition:

Frank T. had an ongoing dispute with one of his neighbors over a stretch of land that both men had claimed for years. The disputes became increasingly hostile when the neighbor repeatedly drove a tractor on the disputed land.

Frank T. decided to teach his neighbor a lesson. He waited until he spotted the neighbor driving onto the disputed land, raised a rifle, and aimed at the neighbor's body. But Frank T. had no experience with guns, his hand slipped on the barrel of the gun, and the shot went wild. Nonetheless, the bullet bounced off a large boulder several feet away and hit the neighbor's body, causing significant injury.
