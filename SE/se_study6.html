The vice-president of a company went to the CEO and said, "We are thinking of starting a new program. It will help us increase profits, but it will also harm the environment." The CEO answered, "I don't care at all about harming the environment. I just want to make as much profit as I can. Let's start the new program." They started the new program. Sure enough, the environment was harmed.
