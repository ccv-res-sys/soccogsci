Harm condition

The vice-president of a company went to the chairman and said, "We are thinking of starting a new program. It will help us increase profits, but it will also harm the environment." The chairman answered, "I don't care at all about harming the environment. I just want to make as much profit as I can. Let's start the new program." They started the new program. Sure enough, the environment was harmed.

Harm condition: Failed Desire Manipulation 1 (see footnote 5)

The vice-president of a company went to the chairman and said, "We are thinking of starting a new program. It will help us increase profits, but it will also harm the environment." The chairman answered, "I truly feel terrible about harming the environment. But it's crucial that we increase profits. Let's start the new program." They started the new program. Sure enough, the environment was harmed.


Harm condition: Failed Desire Manipulation 2 (see footnote 5)

The vice-president of a company went to the chairman and said, "We are thinking of starting a new program. It will help us increase profits, but it will also harm the environment." The chairman paused and thought to himself, I feel terrible about harming the environment. But it's very important that we increase profits. The chairman then turned to the vice-president and said, "Let's start the new program." They started the new program. Sure enough, the environment was harmed.
