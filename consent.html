Informed Consent

You are invited to participate in a research project conducted by Bertram F. Malle at the Social Cognitive Science Research Center, Brown University. This study is part of a larger project that examines how people think about human behavior.

If you decide to participate, you will be asked to read a number of stories and answer questions about them. You may also be asked to complete one or more self-description questionnaires. This study takes 45 minutes to complete, and you will receive $10 of monetary compensation. We cannot guarantee that you will personally receive any benefits from this research, but we benefit greatly from your participation.

There are no anticipated risks in this study, and you should not expect to receive any direct benefits. To maintain confidentiality, we will assign all your data from this experiment a numerical code. Your data record will not be connected to your identity. Please note that complete confidentiality can never be guaranteed when information is transmitted over the internet.

If you have read this form and have decided to participate please understand that your participation in this study is entirely voluntary and you have the right to withdraw your consent or discontinue participation for any reason at any time without penalty and without prejudice to your relations with the Department of Cognitive, Linguistic, and Psychological Sciences or Brown University.

If you have any questions regarding this study please contact us at scsrc@brown.edu. If you have questions about your rights as a participant please contact Human Research Protection Program, Brown University, (401) 863-3050.

You may print a copy of this consent for your records.
