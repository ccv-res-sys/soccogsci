Synchrony, Empathy, and Common Ground
Prepared by Kay Brick
  • Levenson & Reuf
    • empathic physiological response
    • projection during therapy - a method for getting rid of hostilities? (only possible if you put person back into original traumatic state; most hostility is cumulative)
    • excurse: catastrophic shift model - applies to borderline personality (sudden violence) disorder and bulimia (binging and purging)
  • Negative emotional states in relationships
    • bad mood - want to act out (counterregulation necessary to prevent escalation
    • negative emotions are all encompassing, they draw you in
    • negative states = emergency states, scripted behavior; positive states have a broad acceptible range
    • negativity effect within and between people
  • Research by John Gottmann and colleagues: physiology and emotional excahnge in married couples.
    • Findings on unhappy couples: physiological linkage, escalation, unwillingness to "close" conflicts
    • Findings on happy couples: ability to listen to the other rant and willingness to drop insignificant disagreements
    • Tentative finding: Men may stonewall because physiological response is already at their personal ceiling
  • Development and treatment of emotional/agression problems
    • so much to learn (how to interact with others) -- where can children learn it?
    • DARE has no effect -- only addresses one domain of life and does not help internalize new norms
    • If you lack capacity to recognize or even acknowledge others' emotions, you won't be wiling to help and have little inhibition to hurt
    • A continuum: empathy..........neutrality..........blind harm
  • Batson's empathy-altruism hypothesis
    • in 20% condition, people work even harder. why?
      • really want to help OR
      • prove to yourself that you are really "good"
    • have to have empathy and recognition of need to help
    • Big Thought: does it really matter if altruism is due to empathetic or egoistic reasonings? What is important, is that help is given!
