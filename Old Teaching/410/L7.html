Personal vs. Impersonal Causality

Personal Causality Impersonal Causality
purposive, intentional action all the rest
causes --> intention --> action no intention involved
  • Equifinality

    • Note that Heider briefly discusses that an intention is not why the person is trying; why he or she is trying is because of the person's reasons or motives. This concept of reasons has been ignored for many years (but see last topic below).
