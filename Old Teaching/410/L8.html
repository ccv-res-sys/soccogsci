Actor-observer asymmetries
(May 28)

Preliminaries

  • Actor: perceives self; Observer: perceives other
  • Asymmetries
    1. disagreements (conflict)
    2. relative accuracy
  • Domains: attention, explanations, trait inferences, behavior predictions, judgments

Behavior prediction

SHRAUGER et al. (1996)
  • Prediction: target [self], mother, peer --> target
  • Measure: accuracy "hits"; kappa
  • Events: rated for desirability, controllability, ambiguousness, base-rate extremity
  • ? "predicting against base rates"

The Classic Paper

JONES & NISBETT (1972)
  • Actor: situation > disposition; Observer: disposition > situation
  • Problems with the claim: explanation vs. inference? intentional vs. unintentional? positive vs. negative? person vs. disposition
  • Processes:
    Information Information Processing
    Effect data (experiences) Salience (act vs. environm.)
    Cause data (intentions, history) (demands are universal)
