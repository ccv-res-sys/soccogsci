David Hume

  • Born 1711 in Edinburgh, died 1776

  • Never offered a professorship, worked as historian, librarian, essayist

  • Empiricistófundamentally, he offered a psychology of knowledge, morality, religion

  • Foundation of knowledge: impressions and ideas

  • Skeptical analysis of causality and induction

  • Empirical theory of moral sentiments
