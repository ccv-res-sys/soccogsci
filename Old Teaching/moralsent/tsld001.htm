Aristoteles

  • Born 384 BC in Greece, died 322 BC

  • Teacher of Alexander

  • Not seen as a great philosopher during his life time, but one of the greatest in the history of philosophy

  • Foundational contributions to logic, rhetoric, philosophy of science, classification and taxonomy, analysis of language and concepts, metaphysics, ethics, philosophy of mind, political philosophy ...

  • Conceptual analysis of (folk psychology of) intentional action, voluntariness, responsibility.
