Woodward
•Infants were habituated to a hand reaching to and grasping one of two toys. 
•Test events after habituation:
–“new object/old path” event:  hand reached to the old location and thus now grasped a different object. 
–“old object/new path” event: hand grasped the same object as in habitutation, but it was now in the other location. 
•If infants perceived the habituation movement in terms of the hand’s association with the target object, then they should look longer at the new object/old path event because there the hand is associated with a new object. 
•Indeed, 5- and 9- month infants looked longer at the new object/old path event.
