Gergely et al.
•Presented infants with computerized 2-dimensional displays of circles involved in intentional-like motion. 
•Infants were habituated to a circle moving up to and then over a wall-like barrier, then back down across the barrier to join up with a second circle. 
•Two test events after habituation
–“direct”: barrier removed, first circle moves directly in a straight line to link up with the second circle
–“indirect”: barrier removed, first circle moves in the same path as in habituation (although the barrier no longer intervened). 
•9- and 12-month-old infants, but not 6-month-olds, looked longer at the indirect test event. 
•Authors’ interpretation: infants perceived the moving circle as intentional
