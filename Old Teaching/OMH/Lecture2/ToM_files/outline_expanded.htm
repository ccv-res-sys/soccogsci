Theory of mind
Multiple terms (others: folk psychology, naïve theory of action, folk theory of behavior, lay theories, …)
Core: What allows humans to perceive and reason about mental states in self & others.
Conceptual framework
Mental processes
Related: empathy, perspective taking
Broadest term: mental state inference
Components, functions, development, deficiencies, evolution.

Functions and Uses
Recognizing animates & intentional agents; recognizing intentional behavior
Interpreting intentional behavior (goals, desires, intentions, perceptions, beliefs)
Interpreting unintentional behavior (e.g., emotions, mental lapses)
Explaining, predicting behavior
(Moral) evaluation of behavior and agents
