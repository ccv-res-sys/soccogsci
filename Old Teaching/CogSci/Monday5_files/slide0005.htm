Power of Simulation
•Scott Frey:
–Imagining grabbing stick in 12 rotated positions
–Actually grabbing stick in 12 rotated positions
–Choice of wrist orientation and timing are highly similar
•Jean Decety:
–Observing other person’s action or imagining own action (e.g., stapling a paper)
–Actually performing same action
–Similar activation of secondary motor cortex and prefrontal cortex
