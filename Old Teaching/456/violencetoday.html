These headings are approximate, and the seminar is flexible; don't worry if your presentation doesn't match up perfectly. The only concern really, is time - plan to limit your presentation to 10 minutes including any question/answer sessions, and be prepared to end it earlier if the need arises.

What counts as a project?

  • A collage or poster on a particular issue
  • A short video clip (no more than 5 minutes)
  • Presentation of newspaper excerpts1 on a particular topic
  • A short presentation on a controversial article (no more than 5 minutes)
  • A short presentation on a program or activity that may stop violence
  • A joint statement (political or psychological) by the group on a particular issue
  • etc.

1 Because of copyright issues, you are not allowed to make copies of the excerpts for every student.
