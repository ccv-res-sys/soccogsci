Question 6:

In March 1990, CBS Morning News reported a startling finding, namely, that stretching before engaging in physical activity can harm athletes. The results of a large study showed that people who always stretch a moderate amount suffer no fewer injuries than those who never stretch; and those who stretch deeply actually suffer more injuries than those who never stretch.

CBS also went into Central Park and interviewed joggers, those who stretched and those who didn’t, asking what they thought about those data.

Predict the joggers’ reactions to this report, using a dissonance-theory perspective. In your narrative, make clear which group (the stretchers or the non-stretchers) will experience dissonance and why, and list a few ways by which they might reduce their dissonance.

Length: No more than 1/2 page. (35 points)
