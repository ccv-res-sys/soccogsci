Tips for Studying From the Reading Packet

  1. For each of the empirical articles in the reader, try to summarize the main findings in 1-3 sentences.

  2. For each of the theoretical articles, try to summarize the authors's position or claims.

  3. Be sure to understand the following concepts:
    self-concept, self-schema, self-esteem, dissonance, dissonance reduction, self-affirmation, self-awareness [the state], self-consciousness [the trait], self-enhancement, independent vs. interdependent self-conception, intentional vs. unintentional, intention vs. intentionality, reason explanations vs. cause explanations, theory of mind [= folk theory of mind and behavior], action parsing, private vs. communicative explanations, the dispositionist vs. situationist position in the person-situation debate, individual differences.
