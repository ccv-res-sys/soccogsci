Clarifications Regarding the Final Exam

Q2:

"positive glow" is just another term for positive regard. The authors claim that Japanese people have positive self-regard; that's the claim they are trying to defend.

Q4:

The goal is for you to come up with an actor-observer asymmetry that is different from the ones in the two papers we read. As long as it's an asymmetry, as long as you correctly use the concepts of "actor" and "observer," and as long as you make it sound plausible with a few arguments, almost anything goes.

General:

Don't quote, and don't worry about adding references to your final; if you refer to specific articles, just cite them by author name + year (APA style).

Except for Q2 and Q6, there are no "single right answers." So be crative, but back up your claims.
