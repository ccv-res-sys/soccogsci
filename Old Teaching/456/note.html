Note to Students

I have noticed a sharp decline in attendance, from midterm to first discussion and to today's second discussion, and I am disappointed by it. I am sure that some of you have been sick, have voted, or have had some other important obligations. Nevertheless, those who have been absent, please consider the following thoughts:

First, your absence is disrespectful towards your fellow students who put hours of work into their presentations.

Second, I am taking attendance (it's easy because I know all the faces and names by now). In fairness to those who are present, and especially in fairness to the presenters, I will keep doing it.

Third, you are missing out on excellent presentations and interesting discussions -- unique to this course format and this class. Perhaps a seminar format cannot survive in our system of grades and other external rewards, but we will keep doing it in this course, and you might as well benefit from it as long as it lasts.

Bertram Malle
