Tips for Studying From the Reading Packet

  1. For each of the empirical articles in the reader, try to summarize the main findings in 1-3 sentences.

  2. For each of the theoretical articles, try to summarize the authors's position or claims.

  3. Be sure to understand the following concepts:
    conformity, obedience, compliance, persuasion, systematic vs. heuristic processing, self-sanctioning, social learning, illusory correlation, categorization, stereotype threat, belief in a just world, self-fulfilling prophecy, equal-status contact, reciprocity, differential parental investment, concealed ovulation, short-term vs. long-term mating strategies, sexual vs. emotional jealousy
