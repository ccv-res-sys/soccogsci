Evolutionary Mechnisms in Relationships

At the end of Lecture 8, I briefly previewed the arguments and data that evolutionary social psychologists put forth relating to mechanisms of mate selection, and especially gender differences with respect to these mechanisms (keywords: differential parental investment, concealed ovulation, short-term vs. long-term mating strategies, sexual vs. emotional jealousy). We will discuss these arguments and data next week in our first discussion section.
