Questions on Readings

The day before lecture, students send in questions regarding the assigned readings. These questions can ask for clarification or raise a point of discussion. They are posted the evening before class. In the corresponding lecture, as many of the questions as possible will be answered; most of those not addressed in lecture will be answered on the web (barring ignorance!).
Sometimes questions arise after lecture; if theses are of common interest, they will be posted as well.
