Self and Personality
The self literature allowed social psychologists to be comfortable with
“self-type” individual differences (self-esteem, self-monitoring, self-consciousness, etc.)
universal motives (to belong, to control, to feel good)
But the terminology is often misleading

Self vs. Self-Concept
“…the self has been seen primarily as a causal agent in the regulation of behavior” (p. 322)
But: “collectives are not external to individuals, but instead are authentic and significant aspects of the self” (p. 315)
Mixed statements: “the kind of private or public image that the self seeks” (p. 320).
As they explore how “the strategies that an individual adopts [...] affect his or her self-concept,” (p. 322) they also “examine the influence of motivated strategies on the self” (p. 322).
