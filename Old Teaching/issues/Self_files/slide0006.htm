Affective Self-Regulation
[Not the same as emotion regulation]
•Self-esteem: sociometer theory (Leary); role in decision making, aggression
•Self-enhancement: in beliefs, judgments, predictions (positive illusions, totalitarian ego)
•Consistency motive: dissonance reduction, self-verification (Swann)
•Self-affirmation: can live with inconsistency (Steele)
•Self-evaluation maintenance: growing out of social comparison (Tesser)
