3 According to Rosenthal and Rubin's (1982) "binomial effect size display," a correlation of .71 corresponds to a dichotomous prediction accuracy of 85.5%. To my knowledge, it has not been pointed out that the size of the correlation coefficient also directly translates into its predictive gain over chance. Assuming dichotomous chance predictions at 50%, the accuracy of 85.5% stemming from a .71 correlation corresponds to a (85.5 - 50)/50 = 71% increase in predictive accuracy.
