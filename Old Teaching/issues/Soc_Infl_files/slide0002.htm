Conformity
•= behavior that follows a real or imagined norm (reference group, society, culture)
•Variation: imitating behavior of reference group
•Conditions
–1. Ambiguity (e.g., pitted windshields)
–2. Others perceived as experts (e.g., group think, Jonestown)
–3. Stress (e.g., Invasion from Mars)
–4. Need for affiliation (e.g., Asch situation)
