Research Requirement

Your research document can be (a) a theoretical contribution to relevant research not directly discussed in class, (b) a proposal of empirical research on some aspect of intentionality, (c) a pilot study on a question raised in the seminar. Individual or group projects are acceptable, but group projects require prior discussion with the instructors.

An outline of your research document is due in class November 12, 1997. The final document must be written in APA style and is due on Dec 10. The document's length should be 8-12 pages.

Try to write well. Avoid wordiness. Avoid typical grammatical and word-choice mistakes. Look for help in a style manual or on the following we pages: Begin with a few tips for writing, then look at the University of Oregon Director of Publications general guidelines for writing and a list of "troublesome terms". A large number of resources are available at a collection of Grammar and Style Guides
