We examine the cognitive tools that humans bring to social interaction (often called "social cognition"), especially the capacity to recognize intentional actions, make inferences about other people's mental states, explain behavior, and morally evaluate it. Other topics of interest include the relation between social cognition and language, the structure and function of human values, and people's perception of robot morality.
