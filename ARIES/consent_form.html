Informed Consent

  • RESEARCHER: Bertram F. Malle, Ph.D., Brown University.
  • PURPOSE: The study explores the challenges in daily living experiences of older adults.
  • PROCEDURES: You will be asked to complete an online survey about the challenges and opportunities that older adults face when living in their home and answer a few demographic questions.
  • TIME INVOLVED: The study will take approximately 20 minutes to complete.
  • COMPENSATION: You will receive $10 for your time (various options of payment are described at the end of the survey).
  • RISKS: Some of the questions may make you feel uncomfortable. You may skip any questions you prefer not to answer or do not feel comfortable answering.
  • BENEFITS: There are no direct benefits to you if you agree to be in this research study.
  • CONFIDENTIALITY: To maintain confidentiality, we will assign all your data a numerical code. Your responses will not be connected to your identity. Please note that complete confidentiality can never be guaranteed when information is transmitted over the internet. Brown University staff sometimes review studies like this one to make sure they are being done safely and correctly. If a review of this study takes place, your records may be examined. The reviewers will protect your confidentiality.
  • VOLUNTARY: You do not have to be in this study if you do not want to be. Even if you decide to be in this study, you can change your mind and stop at any time.
  • CONTACT INFORMATION: If you have questions about the research in this study, please email us at scsrc@brown.edu.
  • YOUR RIGHTS: If you have questions about your rights as a research participant, you can contact Brown University’s Human Research Protection Program at 401-863-3050 or email them at IRB@Brown.edu.
