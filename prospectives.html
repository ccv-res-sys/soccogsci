Full Time Lab Manager
Dept. of Cognitive, Linguistic and Psychological Sciences
Brown University, Providence, Rhode Island

The position will manage and contribute to Bertram Malle’s research on moral judgment, mental state inferences, and human-robot interaction. The Lab Manager will participate in all facets of the research program. This includes: Developing and pretesting study materials, helping in design of experiments, recruiting research participants (both from the lab’s community database and from the department’s SONA system), scheduling and testing participants, monitoring and contributing to human subject compliance, programming and managing on-line experiments through Qualtrics and other software environments, operating and managing the lab’s computer systems, apparatus for eye tracking, facial measurement, and physiological assessment. Further duties include managing the lab web page and calendar, training and supervising undergraduate research assistants, and supporting graduate and honors students’ in data collection and analysis.

This job is ideal for obtaining broad research competence before applying to Ph.D. programs or conducting research in other settings. Authorship on publications is likely, and pursuing independent research on related topics is encouraged.

Qualifications:
• BA or BS degree required in psychology, cognitive science, computer science, or related field
• Previous experience as research assistant, lab manager, or comparable position
• Previous experience with developing experimental stimuli, design, planning and execution of empirical studies
• Expertise in data analysis, presentation, and reporting
• Expertise in computer programming (e.g., Python, JavaScript, Matlab)
DESIRED (not necessary)
• Experience with specific software and apparatus used in lab (e.g., Qualtrics, SONA, SPSS, R, eye-tracker, physiological equipment, software for robotics control)
• Interest and expertise in domains of moral psychology, social cognition, human-robot interaction
